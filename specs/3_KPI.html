Scope: applies to every model above. Monitors whether the models stay accurate and worth running.
KPIQuestion it answersCalculationGold table

Modeling cautions. Drop-off time, total amount, and tip are known only after the trip, so exclude them from features when predicting duration, fare, or tips. FHV has no fares or distance, and tips exist only for card payments, so train those models on the matching subsets. There are no driver or passenger IDs, so personalization models such as ALS are not possible. Weather and holidays are not in the TLC files, and adding them usually improves demand and duration models. Spark ML has no built-in ARIMA or Prophet, so time-series forecasting uses lag features with tree or linear models.
Note. These KPIs are based on the four TLC trip datasets and the Taxi Zone Lookup. They were defined without a KPI.md file, so if you have one, the KPI list should be reconciled with it.
