NYC TLC Trip Record Field Reference

Field-by-field documentation for each trip-record table published by the NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission: Yellow Taxi, Green Taxi, For-Hire Vehicle (FHV), High-Volume For-Hire Vehicle (HVFHV), and the Taxi Zone Lookup reference table that every dataset joins against.

Yellow Taxi Green Taxi FHV HVFHV Taxi Zone Lookup Shared Codes
Version caveat. Column names, codes, and fees change across years (for example, Airport_fee was added in 2022 and cbd_congestion_fee in 2025). This reference reflects the schemas as commonly published in recent years. Always check the header row of the specific file you are loading and the current TLC data dictionaries before relying on it.

Shared Codes

Lookup values referenced by multiple tables above.

RatecodeID (Yellow and Green)

  • 1 = Standard rate
  • 2 = JFK
  • 3 = Newark
  • 4 = Nassau or Westchester
  • 5 = Negotiated fare
  • 6 = Group ride
  • 99 = Null or unknown

payment_type (Yellow and Green)

  • 0 = Flex Fare trip
  • 1 = Credit card
  • 2 = Cash
  • 3 = No charge
  • 4 = Dispute
  • 5 = Unknown
  • 6 = Voided trip

Data engineering notes

  • Yellow and Green share the same concepts but use different timestamp names (tpep_* vs lpep_*). Rename to a common schema before a UNION.
  • Yellow and Green have trip_type only on Green; add a constant or null column when unioning.
  • FHV has no fare fields. Do not compute revenue from it.
  • HVFHV trip_miles and trip_time are in miles and seconds; Yellow and Green trip_distance is in miles and time is derived from timestamps.
  • Tip and cash handling differs across types: cash tips are never recorded in Yellow or Green, and HVFHV driver pay treatment varies by company.
