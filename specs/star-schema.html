Taxi star schema

The Silver layer of the NYC taxi pipeline: one fact table of trips and four small lookup dimensions. The Gold marts are hourly roll-ups of the fact.

What this star does not have

  • No date or time dimension. pickup_date and pickup_hour are columns of the fact, and every Gold mart groups by pickup hour.
  • Labels are already in the fact. Silver carries the zone, borough and payment label, so most queries need no join. The dimensions keep the lookups in one place.
  • service_type is a degenerate dimension: a plain column and the partition key, with no table of its own.
  • Bad keys are kept, not dropped. A zone that is missing or not in the lookup sets a dq_* flag and the trip stays in the fact.
